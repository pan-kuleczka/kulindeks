Kulindeks is an independent, open-source client for the mStudent university
system used by many Polish universities (made by Simple S.A.). It is a cleaner,
faster alternative built on the same backend.

Features:
* Dashboard — your student card, next class, grade average, outstanding
  payments and unread messages, each tapping through to the right section.
* Timetable — a real calendar with Day / Week / Month views, swipe navigation,
  room locations on a map, iCalendar (.ics) export, and one-tap Nextcloud
  (CalDAV) sync.
* Grades — ECTS-weighted and arithmetic averages, color-coded, per programme
  and semester.
* Finances — dues and scholarships.
* Messages — inbox with a full message view.
* Light, dark and high-contrast themes, a custom accent color, adjustable font
  size, Polish and English, class reminders, and a clear offline indicator that
  keeps showing cached data.

Privacy: the app talks only to your own university's server using your own
login, and stores data on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere else — no
analytics, no ads, no tracking.

Unofficial / independent project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
connected to Simple S.A. or any university. Use it with your own account.
